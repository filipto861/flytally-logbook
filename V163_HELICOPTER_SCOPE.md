# FlyTally v1.63 — Helicopter support

Candidate scope:
- explicit `HELICOPTER` aircraft and regulatory category without separate flight-entry pages,
- helicopter profiles remain type-specific through stored aircraft identity/model,
- Part-FCL PF movement evidence in the universal flight form,
- LAPL(H) FCL.140.H recency evaluated per specific helicopter type,
- FCL.060 helicopter passenger recent experience without automatic cross-type pooling,
- signed proficiency-check evidence as the alternative FCL.140.H route,
- helicopter licences and qualifications in the adaptive Licences workspace,
- SPL recency panel wiring restored alongside Aeroplane and Helicopter recency,
- portable backup/restore includes helicopter recency evidence,
- no automatic revalidation or invented type-rating expiry dates.

Safety boundaries:
- different helicopter types are not pooled automatically;
- unsigned dual/supervised evidence is not credited to FCL.140.H;
- movement credit requires explicit PF evidence;
- certification fingerprint v5 already binds regulatory category, so no new fingerprint version is required;
- existing Aeroplane, ULL and Sailplane semantics remain unchanged.
