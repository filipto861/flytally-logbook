# FlyTally v1.62 — Sailplane / SPL / TMG support

## Scope

v1.62 extends the single FlyTally flight-entry workflow with an explicit regulatory aircraft context instead of inferring legal meaning from an aircraft class alone.

- Aircraft and flight snapshots distinguish `AEROPLANE`, `SAILPLANE`, `ULL`, and `OTHER`.
- Legacy TMG records remain in the existing Part-FCL aeroplane context unless SPL / Part-SFCL is selected explicitly.
- Non-TMG sailplanes record launch method and launch count explicitly. GPS imports never invent launch methods.
- SPL TMG flights record explicit day/night take-off counts independently from landing counts.
- Sailplane flight-time credit uses AIR time when available, with BLOCK as a conservative fallback when AIR is unavailable.
- Flight certification fingerprint v5 binds regulatory category and launch evidence. Fingerprints v1-v4 remain verifiable unchanged.
- Shared-flight materialisation, trash/restore and portable backup preserve the new evidence.
- Portable backup v9 includes SPL proficiency-check evidence.

## Part-SFCL recency

The SPL recency workspace is enabled only for users with an active SPL licence and evaluates explicit certified evidence for:

- SFCL.160(a) sailplane privileges;
- SFCL.160(b) TMG privileges;
- SFCL.160(c) Part-FCL TMG route;
- SFCL.160(e) sailplane and TMG passenger currency;
- SFCL.155(c) launch-method recency;
- sailplane/TMG proficiency-check evidence.

TMG time may contribute to the five-hour SFCL.160(a) total, while the 15 launches and two FI(S) training flights remain restricted to sailplanes excluding TMGs. Self-launch recency may be supported by explicit TMG take-offs as permitted by SFCL.155(c).

## Safety boundaries

- Existing TMG history is never silently reclassified into SPL evidence.
- Launch method is never derived from a GPS track.
- Part-FCL FCL.060 PF movement evidence is kept separate from SPL TMG take-off/landing evidence.
- No new official-looking SPL print template is fabricated in this release; sailplane stored-record presentation is labelled Part-SFCL rather than FCL.050.
- Existing EASA/ULL workflows and historical certification versions remain backward compatible.

## Candidate validation

Release is not considered complete until unit regressions, TypeScript, production build, PostgreSQL acceptance and deployment/runtime checks pass.
