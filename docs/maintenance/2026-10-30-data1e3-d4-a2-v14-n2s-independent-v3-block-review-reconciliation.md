## 3. Single next OWNER policy decision before design V4

**Recommended v13 restore policy V13-I — DESIGN ONLY:** when restoring a positively authenticated signed-v13 backup, do **not** insert its historical `audit_log` rows into `flight_audit_log`. Preserve them in the dedicated immutable imported audit ledger as `legacy_v13_imported`, account-scoped **UNATTACHED**, retaining original signed `source_user_id`, `source_audit_id`, `source_flight_id`, original source digest, event payload and `verified_at_import_only` claim. Restore of eligible separate account/flight entities may still create **new real local native trigger events**, which stay native. All critical ID/owner/equality/cross-digest guards must be atomic under v13 A. Any conflicts, absent required schema, unsupported authenticity or incomplete `pre_restore` => whole restore abort. Do not equate numeric IDs, generate fake event UID for source, overwrite/de-duplicate across distinct signed-v13 digests, or silently discard historical rows. **This specific restore semantic has NOT yet been separately owner-approved.**

Rejected alternative (for explicit contrast): v13 restore only when target native audit is empty; can block routine recovery and does not address nonempty imported ledger, so less compatible with chosen Option 2 and L1. This is a recommendation, not adoption of a new release feature.
